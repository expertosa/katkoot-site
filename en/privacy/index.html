Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · Provider (controller): Experto (اكسبرتو) · Contact: hi@experto.sa

1. Who this is for

Katkoot («كتكوت») is an educational app for children aged 3–12. It is provided by Experto. This policy is written for parents and guardians. A short summary is also in the app (Parents' area → Privacy).

2. The short version

  • The app works offline after setup. Experto runs no account server, and there are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • A parent may optionally sign in with Google or Apple, or create a local account with an optional email. Children never sign in.
  • Everything the app stores stays on this device, and you can delete it at any time. Google or Apple process the sign-in request if that option is used.
  • The app does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.
  • There is no chat, no public profile, no leaderboard and no in-app purchase.

Experto does not collect this data on a server: nothing above is transmitted to Experto. If a parent uses Google or Apple sign-in, that provider processes the sign-in request under its own policy, and we keep only the id, email and name on the device. Android requests the Internet permission solely for this optional parent sign-in. After setup, play and progress work fully offline.

4. Parental consent and the parental gate

A parent completes setup and gives consent before any child profile is created. Optional Google or Apple sign-in is offered only after that consent, and only to the parent. Parent-only areas (dashboard, controls, settings, adding time, deleting data) are behind a parental gate. Children never sign in.

5. Competitions

Competitions are private family challenges. Entries are saved only on the device. There is no upload, public ranking, name display or prize. A parent can mark an entry as approved. Sharing outside the device is not available in this version. If it is ever added, it will be off by default, need a parent's separate consent, and this policy will be updated first.

6. Videos and content

Videos are bundled with the app and play offline. There is no browsing, search, recommendation feed or autoplay. Quran text comes from a verified source and is never altered.

7. Your rights

You can view your child's data (parents' area) and delete it at any time. Deletion on the device is immediate. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing on our side to access or delete. For any question, write to hi@experto.sa. You may also contact the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) or your local regulator.

8. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage on the device, protected by the operating system's app sandbox and the device lock you set. Uninstalling the app removes it.

9. Changes

Before any change to what the app stores or sends, we will update this policy, tell parents in the app, and ask for new consent where required.
