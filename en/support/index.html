Frequently asked questions

Does the app need the Internet? Play and progress work offline. Optional parent sign-in with Google or Apple needs the Internet only at that moment; nothing is sent to Experto servers.

How do I open the parents' area? Tap the lock at the top of the screen, then answer the simple multiplication question (the parental gate).

How do I set screen time? Parents' area → Parental controls: daily limit, bedtime, break reminder and section switches.

How do I delete my child's data? Parents' area → the delete icon next to the child's name, or delete the app to remove everything.

Are there ads or purchases? No ads, no in-app purchases, no chat and no leaderboards.

I found a mistake in the content or in a Quran text. Please email us with a screenshot right away; we review every report.
